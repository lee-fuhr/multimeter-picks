How we choose our picks

We don&#8217;t claim to run our own lab tests. Instead, we rely on the collective wisdom of thousands of owners on forums, Amazon, and tool-review sites, plus published evaluations from respected industry sources. What actually breaks on a multimeter? We look for consistent complaints about flimsy leads, unreliable fuses, and displays that die in bright sunlight. Meters that survive years in a service van without those failures earn our attention. We also weigh safety certifications (CAT ratings) more heavily than fancy features. A meter with a genuine CAT III rating from a known brand like Fluke or Klein will protect you far better than a cheap no-name with a higher count. Reputation, real-world failure data, and ease of getting replacement leads or fuses all factor into our final picks.
